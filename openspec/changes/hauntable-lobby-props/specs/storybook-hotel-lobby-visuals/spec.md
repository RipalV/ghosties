## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Hauntable props read as lobby furniture
The three hauntable props SHALL appear as storybook hotel furniture with readable silhouettes at fixed world positions. Idle presentation SHALL not overwhelm the lobby. Casting and resolve reactions SHALL stay mildly spooky and comedic for ages 7+, with no death, injury, weapons, or realistic horror. Prop visuals SHALL remain legible under the existing camera zoom steps.

#### Scenario: Props visible as furniture
- **WHEN** the lobby is shown
- **THEN** the reception bell, crooked portrait, and drafty fireplace or curtains are visible as distinct silhouettes
- **AND** they do not permanently display dense text labels over the play area

#### Scenario: Resolve reaction stays family-friendly
- **WHEN** a prop plays its combo resolve reaction
- **THEN** the effect remains suitable for ages 7+
- **AND** it contains no death, injury, combat, blood, or weapons
