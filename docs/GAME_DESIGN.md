# Game Design Snapshot

## Audience and tone

- Ages 7+
- Mischievous and comedic with light spooky tension
- No death, injury, combat, or violence

## Prototype loop

1. Move around the venue while normally invisible.
2. Observe an NPC and test a basic scare.
3. Read whether the reaction is high, medium, or ineffective.
4. Avoid repeating the same scare because novelty decreases.
5. Build fear through readable stages.
6. Unlock possession and use it for two funny helper actions in a later iteration.

## Product direction

- Browser-first and installable later as a PWA
- Solo offline prototype first
- Two-to-four-player private co-op later
- Light individual awards rather than sabotage-based competition
